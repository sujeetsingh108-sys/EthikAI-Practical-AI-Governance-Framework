# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 – Clause-wise Documentation Matrix

## Clause 4 – Context of the Organization

| Clause | Requirement | Document | Mandatory | Justification | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|----------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| 4.1 | The organization shall determine external and internal issues that are relevant to its purpose and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcomes of the AI Management System (AIMS). | Organization Context Analysis |Recommended| The effectiveness of an AI Management System depends on understanding the environment in which AI systems are designed, developed, deployed, and operated. This document demonstrates that the organization has identified all relevant internal and external issues—including business objectives, organizational strategy, AI maturity, technology stack, applicable regulations, market expectations, ethical considerations, cybersecurity threats, and stakeholder concerns—that may influence AI governance. It provides the basis for defining the AIMS scope, identifying risks and opportunities, and establishing governance objectives. | To document the organization's internal and external operating environment that influences the planning, implementation, operation, and continual improvement of the AI Management System. The report establishes a baseline understanding of business objectives, AI strategy, regulatory landscape, technological capabilities, organizational maturity, and market conditions. It serves as a key input for defining the AIMS scope, identifying AI-related risks and opportunities, establishing governance objectives, and ensuring governance decisions remain aligned with organizational priorities.|
| 4.2 | Understand needs and expectations of interested parties | Interested Parties Register | Yes | Identifies stakeholders and their AI-related expectations. | Stakeholder register, legal requirements register |
| 4.3 | Determine the scope of the AI Management System | AIMS Scope Document | Yes | Defines organizational boundaries, AI systems covered, exclusions, and interfaces. | Approved scope statement |
| 4.4 | Establish, implement, maintain and continually improve the AIMS | AI Management System Manual / Process Architecture | Recommended | Shows how processes interact to form the AI Management System. | Process maps, governance framework |
