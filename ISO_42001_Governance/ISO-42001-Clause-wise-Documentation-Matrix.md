# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 – Clause-wise Documentation Matrix

## Clause 4 – Context of the Organization

| Clause | Requirement | Document | Mandatory | Purpose | Justification |
|--------|-------------|----------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| 4.1 | The organization shall determine the internal and external issues that are relevant to its purpose and strategic direction and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcomes of the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). These issues shall be monitored and reviewed periodically to ensure their continued relevance.| Organization Context Analysis |Recommended| To document the organization's internal and external operating environment that influences the planning, implementation, operation, and continual improvement of the AI Management System. The report establishes a baseline understanding of business objectives, AI strategy, regulatory landscape, technological capabilities, organizational maturity, and market conditions. It serves as a key input for defining the AIMS scope, identifying AI-related risks and opportunities, establishing governance objectives, and ensuring governance decisions remain aligned with organizational priorities.| | The effectiveness of an AI Management System depends on understanding the environment in which AI systems are designed, developed, deployed, and operated. This document demonstrates that the organization has identified all relevant internal and external issues—including business objectives, organizational strategy, AI maturity, technology stack, applicable regulations, market expectations, ethical considerations, cybersecurity threats, and stakeholder concerns—that may influence AI governance. It provides the basis for defining the AIMS scope, identifying risks and opportunities, and establishing governance objectives.|
| 4.2 | The organization shall determine the interested parties that are relevant to the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS), identify their relevant needs and expectations, and determine which of those requirements become applicable obligations for the organization. The organization shall monitor and review these interested parties and their requirements periodically to ensure that the AIMS remains aligned with evolving legal, regulatory, contractual, ethical, and business expectations. Interested parties may include customers, regulators, employees, suppliers, AI developers, business partners, investors, certification bodies, government agencies, end users, and society at large. | Stakeholder Register | Yes |The Interested Parties Register is a foundational governance document that systematically identifies all stakeholders who influence or are influenced by the organization's AI systems and AI Management System (AIMS). It captures each stakeholder's role, interests, expectations, applicable legal or contractual obligations, level of influence, communication requirements, and governance responsibilities. The register provides a structured mechanism for translating stakeholder expectations into governance requirements, risk management activities, operational controls, AI policies, objectives, and compliance obligations. It should include stakeholder categorization (internal and external), applicable regulations, contractual commitments, privacy obligations, ethical expectations, cybersecurity requirements, reporting obligations, communication channels, review frequency, and ownership. This document is maintained throughout the lifecycle of the AIMS and is reviewed whenever significant organizational, regulatory, contractual, or technological changes occur. Information from this register directly supports scope determination (Clause 4.3), AI risk assessments (Clause 6.1), governance objectives (Clause 6.2), communication planning (Clause 7.4), operational controls (Clause 8), and management review activities (Clause 9.3). | **Although ISO/IEC 42001 does not prescribe a document named "Interested Parties Register," documented information is necessary to demonstrate that the organization has systematically identified relevant stakeholders and evaluated their needs and expectations. Certification auditors typically request objective evidence showing how stakeholder requirements were identified, prioritized, monitored, and translated into governance activities. This register provides traceability between stakeholder expectations and AI governance controls, ensuring that legal requirements, contractual obligations, ethical principles, privacy expectations, transparency commitments, and regulatory obligations are consistently addressed throughout the AI lifecycle. It also demonstrates that the organization proactively manages relationships with regulators, customers, employees, suppliers, AI users, and other affected parties rather than reacting only after issues arise. During certification audits, this document enables auditors to verify that governance decisions are aligned with stakeholder expectations and that changes in regulatory frameworks, customer requirements, or societal expectations are systematically monitored and incorporated into the AIMS. Maintaining this register significantly strengthens governance maturity, supports continual improvement, and provides clear evidence that stakeholder engagement is embedded within the organization's AI governance framework.|
| 4.3 | Determine the scope of the AI Management System | AIMS Scope Document | Yes | Defines organizational boundaries, AI systems covered, exclusions, and interfaces. | Approved scope statement |
| 4.4 | Establish, implement, maintain and continually improve the AIMS | AI Management System Manual / Process Architecture | Recommended | Shows how processes interact to form the AI Management System. | Process maps, governance framework |
