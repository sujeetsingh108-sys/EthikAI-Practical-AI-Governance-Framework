<h1>Purpose</h1>

This document identifies the internal and external issues that influence MedVision Diagnostics Pvt. Ltd.'s ability to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve its Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS).

The AI Management System supports the responsible development, deployment, and monitoring of AI-powered diagnostic solutions while ensuring patient safety, regulatory compliance, ethical AI practices, and clinical reliability.
